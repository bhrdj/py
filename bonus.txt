Here are some additional jokes for your enjoyment:

1. Why don't skeletons fight each other?
   They don't have the guts, and neither does their friend because he hasn't got a stomach for it.

   Because he was outstanding in his field!

5. What do you get when you cross a snowman and a vampire?
